## 1. Layer Comparison

- Layer 7 (Application): HTTP, DNS, TLS, SSH, WebSockets.
- Layer 4 (Transport): TCP (reliable, ordered), UDP (fast, connectionless).
- Layer 3 (Network): IP addressing, routing, ICMP (ping).
- Layer 2 (Data Link): Ethernet frames, MAC addresses, switches.
