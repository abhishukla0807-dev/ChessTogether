## 1. Immediate FinOps Wins

- Terminate unattached EBS volumes and idle NAT Gateways.
- Purchase AWS Savings Plans or Reserved Instances for baseline capacity (saves up to 72%).
- Use S3 Intelligent-Tiering to automatically archive aged objects.
