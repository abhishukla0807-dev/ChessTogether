## 1. Managed SaaS vs Self-Hosted

- Self-hosted (Prometheus/Grafana): Zero license costs, full data control, but requires operations overhead.
- SaaS (Datadog): Instant setup, turnkey integrations, but can become extremely expensive at scale.
