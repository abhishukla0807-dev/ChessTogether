## 1. How Error Budgets Work

- If SLO is 99.9%, your error budget is 0.1%.
- Budget remaining: Deploy experimental features and make major refactors.
- Budget depleted: Freeze feature deployments; focus 100% of engineering effort on reliability and bug fixes.
