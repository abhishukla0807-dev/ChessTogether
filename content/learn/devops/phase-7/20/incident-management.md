## 1. Incident Roles

- Incident Commander (IC): Coordinates response, delegates investigation tasks, maintains calm.
- Operations Lead: Directly executes diagnostic commands and mitigation steps.
- Communications Lead: Updates status pages and stakeholders every 15 minutes.
