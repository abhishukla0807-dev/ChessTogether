## 1. SLA vs SLO

- SLO: Internal goal (e.g. 99.9%).
- SLA: External contract with penalty (e.g. 99.5% with 10% credit refund if breached).
- Never make your internal SLO equal to your customer-facing SLA—keep a safety buffer.
