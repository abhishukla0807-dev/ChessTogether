## 1. The Gambit Idea

- Moves: 1.d4 d5 2.c4.
- Key Idea: White offers the c4 pawn. If Black accepts (2...dxc4 - Accepted), White plays e4 to occupy the full center.
- If Black declines (2...e6 - Declined, or 2...c6 - Slav), White maintains relentless spatial pressure.
