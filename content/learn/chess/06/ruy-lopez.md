## 1. The Spanish Setup

- Moves: 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bb5.
- Key Idea: White pins or threatens the c6 Knight which defends Black's central e5 pawn.
- Black's classic replies: 3...a6 (Morphy Defense), 3...Nf6 (Berlin Defense).
